Know your produce

We use compost, crop rotation and drip irrigation. Vegetables are harvested in the morning and packed the same day. Store leafy greens in the fridge and use within five days.

Fruit ripens on the plant, not in a crate. That gives better sweetness and aroma. It is best eaten within three days of delivery.

Our cows are health-checked regularly. Milk is cooled within an hour of milking and delivered chilled. We recommend boiling milk before drinking.

We strain it but never heat it, which keeps its natural enzymes and flavour. It may crystallise over time; warm the jar gently in water to restore it.
